News

We’re so happy to share that we were awarded 2nd prize at the Kitty Weinberger Competition!

It’s been quite a journey: just two weeks after returning from Madrid, we threw ourselves into preparing our French programme for the first time — and introducing a new member of the ensemble: the viola da gamba.

Now it’s time for a little breather… but of course, we already have new plans and a very long to-do list waiting for us.

Thank you to everyone who supported us along the way. On to the next adventure! ✨
